Development-only analysis of eighty settings in ten base groups. No new targets, TTN builds, synthesis or GNN training. Definitions and input/code hashes were fixed before fitting; this is not an independent validation of a new method.

## Local merge mechanisms

544 parent–merge pairs for 480 unique merged candidates. Mixed dominates in 352 pairs; binary dominates in 112 pairs. All possible B-pool source parents were retained; these are correlated observations, not independent samples.

local_pairs.json records removed leaf groups, changed common ranks, local matrix sizes, reduced construction spectra, discarded weights, tolerance and representation-fidelity changes. These describe independently truncated representations; neither a pure arity causal effect nor complete physical Schmidt spectra is claimed.

The analysis ends here. Any proposed feature-based strategy needs new held-out targets and a genuinely measured adaptive pipeline. Do not automatically launch a GNN or another target sweep.
