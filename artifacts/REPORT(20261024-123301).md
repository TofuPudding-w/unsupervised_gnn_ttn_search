## Accounting and interpretation

3359 actual fresh candidate evaluations, 3359 synthesis starts; 80/80 completed online target pairs and 80/80 offline diagnostic targets.

K=4 is primary; K=1/2 are saved checkpoints of the same actual run. Both methods construct 24 candidates and pay for four evaluations including retries and repeated shortlist construction. Whole-method times include fresh SCENT/pool generation, scoring, worker startup, verification and I/O. Target creation and audit freeze are separate. One run per method/target is not a robust timing replication.

Offline reuse is bookkeeping for pool diagnostics only, never a free online evaluation. Per-seed, family and alpha/gamma identities remain in paired_results.json for full stratified inspection. Pooled comparisons are not independent samples. Missing results remain missing. Do not infer arity causality from B/M differences: the generators also differ.

This experiment ends here. Continuous small effects and original practical thresholds are both reported; neither a positive hidden reference nor a small selected-circuit advantage automatically authorizes GNN training or another sweep.
