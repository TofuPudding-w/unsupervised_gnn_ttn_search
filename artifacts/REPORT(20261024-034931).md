## Accounting and limits

All selected edges and serialized tensors were frozen before these compilation labels. The original binary and each refinement are compiled independently. Refinement singular spectra, numerical-zero thresholds, local residuals, block dimensions, and common-state build retries are retained in case.json and inputs.npz.

Each variant runs in a fresh process. Peak RSS (Linux KiB converted to MiB) includes imports, loading, synthesis and statevector verification, not solely compiler allocations; baseline and phase peaks are in record.json. Process time includes startup and I/O. One timing sample, fixed execution order: these measurements do not establish a speedup.

These are paired local regroupings of the same approximate states, not independently optimized higher-arity TTNs, global optima, or an online selection benchmark. Cases sharing a target are not independent targets. Failed cases remain visible; no additional variants are generated.
